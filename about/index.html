About

One person, from the first conversation to long after launch.

I'm Dave, an independent builder based in Cape Town. I work with businesses locally and around the world.

You deal with the person who works out the problem and builds the solution, and who is still there after launch. There are no account managers and no handoffs between teams.

My background runs from data work for very large companies, including leading Africa's largest SAP Retail rollout, to phone apps with real users that I built and grew on my own. That mix matters: I know what it takes to get software from an idea to something people use every day, and to keep it working.

What I'm best at is listening to how a business really runs, spotting what would help, and building something people actually use. Often that is smaller and simpler than owners expect.

How I work

  • I start with how your business works, not with a list of features.
  • I explain things in plain language. You get the technical choices that affect your decision, and none of the rest.
  • You get a defined scope, price and timeline in writing before anything is built.
  • I say so when something isn't worth building, or when it needs more than it first seems.
  • I stay on after launch, if you want me to, to run it, fix what breaks and keep improving it.
The tools I build with

React Native, React and Next.js, Python, Node.js, Supabase and Postgres, SAP S/4HANA data quality, data migration and ETL, and language-model integration. I pick what suits the problem, and I'll explain the choice if it affects you.
