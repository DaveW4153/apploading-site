07Observer Hub · swim ratification platform

Evidence sealed the moment the swim ends.

Problem
Ratifying a long-distance swim depends on what one observer saw, usually written up by hand after the swim, then reviewed by a body that has to take it largely on trust.
Built
Observer Hub: an offline-first capture app the observer opens from a link on the boat (check-ins, feeds, attire, incidents, each timestamped and GPS-tagged), plus a review hub where the ratifying body queries the observer, records its decision and issues the certificate.
Result
Saves the observer 6 hours of write-up after every swim. Evidence is assembled and SHA-256 sealed the moment capture ends, and nothing in it can be quietly edited: a correction is a new version with a reason.
