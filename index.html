Featured: a working demonstration

A website can help win the booking. A guest companion can help look after the guest.

A lodge owner told me they might need an app, but they weren't sure what it would be. I didn't start with a spec. I looked at how the business works, then built one idea to show what it could mean.

The starting point
An accommodation website, and an open conversation about whether the lodge might need an app. No brief and no feature list.
The opportunity I saw
Guests have questions before they arrive and while they stay, and most are answered one message at a time. Much of that could live in the guest's own pocket.
What I designed and built
A personal link for each booking that opens a guest app in the phone's browser. Nothing to download. The home screen changes as the stay moves: preparing, arriving, staying, leaving, remembering.

  1. 01 / 04

    Welcome and preparation

    A personal link for each booking. It counts down to arrival and gives a short before-you-go checklist.

  2. 02 / 04

    Arrival day

    Directions, the address, a nudge to arrive before sunset, and check-in questions that go to the host in one tap.

  3. 03 / 04

    Wildlife passport

    Guests tick off the animals they have seen and add their own photos, which stay on their phone.

  4. 04 / 04

    Trip memories

    At the end, the app makes a memories card on the guest's phone: a real image to keep or share.

Working demonstration

Real screens from the app, captured on a phone-sized screen. The lodge's name, host, address, contact details and photos are replaced with invented details and freely licensed stock photos. The guests are sample people.

What a guest can do today

  • Count down to arrival and work through a short before-you-go checklist
  • See the lodge forecast, sunrise, sunset and moon. Without signal, the app shows the last saved forecast and says so
  • Get directions and drive times, and find food, fuel, pharmacies and hospitals nearby
  • Find emergency numbers, and message or call the host in one tap
  • Keep a wildlife passport: tick off sightings, add photos that stay on their phone, earn small badges
  • Make a trip memories card as a real image to keep or share
  • Ask for extras such as a transfer or a braai pack. The host confirms price and availability
  • Use it in English or German, and keep using it without signal once opened

What it could mean for an owner

  • Fewer of the same questions arriving one by one before a stay
  • One place to send every guest, with the host's contact details one tap away
  • A tidy way for guests to ask for extras
  • A personal touch that guests can keep after they leave

To be clear: this is a working demonstration, not a deployed product. It isn't connected to a booking system, and I haven't measured any time savings. The points above are what the idea is for, not results.

Delivered system

For an open-water swimming ratification body

The paperwork is done by the time the swim ends.

What was hard
After every swim, the observer on the boat had to write up everything they had seen before the swim could be assessed.
What I built
An app that works offline on the boat. The observer records check-ins, feeds and incidents with time and location as they happen, and the evidence is assembled and sealed the moment the swim ends. The ratifying body reviews it and issues certificates in the same system.
What changed
It saves the observer six hours of write-up after every swim. I built it in eight days.
