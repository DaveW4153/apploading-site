Data quality engine

55 rules. 200k records.
Daily. In production.

Problem

An enterprise SAP S/4HANA migration needed live master-data validation — no vendor tool covered the business's specific rule logic, and nothing caught bad records before they hit the new system.

Built

A Python and Streamlit data quality engine — 55 custom rules running daily against live master data, scoring records and routing every issue to a business owner, with a GPT-powered layer that turns failed-rule patterns into a plain-English root-cause report.

Result

171,000 issues caught before go-live, across 993 monitored columns. No vendor. No off-the-shelf tooling. Built, deployed and running.

04
Ocean-swim operations platform

Coordinating boats and crew,
safely, from a phone at sea.

Problem

A Cape Town ocean-swim business was coordinating boats, crew and swimmers by phone and spreadsheet — no shared view of who was where, and no way for a family to know if a swimmer was safe mid-swim.

Built

A real-time operations platform — mission scheduling around weather windows, 11 incident types tracked, automatic alerts if a boat goes quiet, and a live tracking page that goes well beyond a boat blip on a map: position against the planned route, pace and stroke-rate charts, water temperature, and a running check-in feed from the crew.

Result

Live since July 2026. Built for cold, wet gloves on a moving deck — not office UX.
