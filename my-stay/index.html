Look after every guest, from booking to goodbye. In an app with your lodge's name on it.

What your guest gets

One link. The whole stay.

The home screen changes as the trip moves along, so a guest always sees what matters today. Everything shown here works in the demo.

01 Before they travel

They arrive ready, not full of questions.

From the day they book, your guest has a countdown, a plan and the answers to what they would otherwise message you about.

  • A countdown to arrival, with their dates and party
  • A before-you-go checklist: arrival time, gate access, passport and entry rules, insurance
  • A packing list built for that month's weather
  • Routes and drive times from every nearby airport, with directions and Waze
  • Flight details saved to their calendar, with reminders for check-in and when to leave
  • The forecast at your lodge, with sunrise, sunset, the moon and storm and UV warnings

02 Arrival day

The first evening goes the way you planned it.

On the day, the app turns into a guide to reaching you in daylight and settling in, without a call from the gate.

  • A check that they will reach you before sunset, which they can send you in one tap
  • Directions to the lodge and an "I have arrived" tap at the gate
  • Check-in and check-out times, Wi-Fi, gate access and house rules, answered in the app
  • Anything you have not filled in becomes a ready-written WhatsApp question to you
  • What is included in their suite, and your WhatsApp, phone and email one tap away

03 During the stay

Every day, they know what is worth doing.

The app plans around the weather and the light, shows what is nearby, and turns the wildlife into something guests collect.

  • A plan for each day: first light, the heat of the day, golden hour, after dark
  • A map and lists of places to eat, shops, fuel, pharmacies, hospitals and sights, with opening hours and drive times
  • A wildlife guide with photos: how likely each animal is to be seen, how to recognise it and how to behave near it
  • "Help me identify an animal", by kind, size and time of day
  • A wildlife passport with their own photos, a scorecard and discovery badges, and a daily explorer challenge for children
  • Extras they can ask you for: a transfer, a game drive, a bush dinner, a massage
  • An SOS screen: emergency numbers, the nearest medical help and "send my location to my host"

04 Leaving, and after

They leave with something to keep. You keep the guest.

The last screens are about the memory of the stay, and about coming back to you directly.

  • A check-out list, and a leave-by time worked back from their flight home
  • A trip memories card made on their phone from their photos, sightings and badges, to keep or share
  • A prompt to tell you how the stay went, and to leave a review
  • A way to book again, direct with you
  • Photos they choose to send you, with a separate yes before you use one in your marketing
  • Nothing to downloadIt opens from a link in the phone's browser. Guests can add it to their home screen.
  • Their languageEnglish and German today, switched in one tap. French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Chinese and others are added per lodge.
  • Works without signalOnce opened, the guides, checklists, passport and photos keep working in the bush.
  • Private by designSightings and photos stay on the guest's own phone. Location data is removed from every photo.

What it does for your lodge

Fewer of the same questions, one WhatsApp at a time.

Your guests are often first-time visitors, flying a long way, with a lot to ask. Most of the answers are the same for every booking. My stay puts them in the guest's pocket, and leaves your team the conversations that need a person.

I haven't measured time saved or extra revenue, so I won't put a number on either.

Before they arrive
Guests turn up in daylight, packed for the season, with the gate and the check-in sorted, and they already know the rules of the bush.
While they stay
Requests for extras reach you ready written, with the guest's name, dates, party size and time of day. You confirm the price and the timing as you do now.
After they leave

Questions lodge owners ask

Straight answers.

Do guests have to download anything?

No. You send the guest a personal link. It opens in the phone's browser, and they can add it to their home screen if they like.

Does it work without signal?

Once it has been opened, yes. The guides, checklists, lodge details, emergency numbers, passport and photos all keep working. The weather shows the last saved forecast and says so. The map and sending messages need signal.

Does it connect to my booking system?

Not yet. Today you create each guest's link by hand in an owner screen. Importing bookings automatically needs an integration with your booking system, which I would scope with you.

Does it take payments?

No. Extras are requests. They open in the guest's WhatsApp or email, and you confirm the price and availability there.

What happens to guests' photos?

They stay on the guest's own phone and are private by default. A guest can choose to send you a photo from their phone, and separately say whether you may use it in marketing. Photos of sensitive species are never offered for marketing, and the app never records where one was seen.

What does it cost?

There is no set price yet. After a first conversation you get a defined scope, price and timeline in writing, with optional monthly support after launch.

We are not a safari lodge. Does it still fit?

Yes. The wildlife passport becomes a guide to whatever is around you: walks, wine farms, beaches, birds. The rest of the stay works the same way.
