Photo credits

The sample guest app is set up for an invented lodge. Its photos are by the photographers below, shared under free licences on Wikimedia Commons. Each was resized and cropped for the app. None shows the sample lodge, which does not exist, and the photographers do not endorse this app.

When the app is set up for a real lodge, every photo is replaced with the lodge's own.
