      Your part

      What it takes from you

      • Once, at the start

        Confirm the lodge details guests ask about: check-in and check-out, Wi-Fi, gate access, house rules. Choose your favourite places, set up the extras you offer and their prices, and approve the photos and wording.

      • For each booking

        Create the guest's link with their name and dates, and send it. Today you do this in this studio. Later it could happen automatically from your booking system.

      • During stays

        Reply to guests on WhatsApp, as you do now. Requests for extras and questions arrive there, already written out with the guest's name and dates.
